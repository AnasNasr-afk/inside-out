Privacy Policy

Inside Out · Last updated: June 2026

Your child's privacy matters to us

1 Who We Are

Inside Out ("we", "our", "us") operates the Inside Out therapy companion application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information about you and your child when you use the App.

2 What Data We Collect

Data Type What It Is
Account data Parent name, email address, phone number
Child profile Child name, age, medical case description
Session data Conversation transcripts with Poly, emotion classifications, task progress
Session reports AI-generated summaries of therapy sessions shared with your specialist
Device data App version, device type (for debugging only — no personal identifiers)

We do not collect voice recordings. Speech is transcribed on-device or via the system speech API and only the text is processed.

3 How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalise the Poly AI companion experience
  • To generate session reports and share them with your linked specialist
  • To allow your specialist to monitor your child's therapy progress
  • To improve the App's performance and fix bugs
We never use your child's data to train AI models or for advertising purposes.

4 Who We Share Data With

Your data is shared only with:

  • Your linked specialist — session reports and task progress are visible to the specialist assigned to your child's account
  • OpenAI — conversation text is sent to OpenAI's API (GPT-4o) to generate Poly's responses. OpenAI processes this under their API data usage policy and does not use API data for model training by default
  • Google Cloud — text-to-speech synthesis uses Google Cloud TTS. Only Poly's response text is sent, not user input

We do not sell your data to any third party.

5 Data Storage & Security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Session report cache is stored locally on your device using encrypted shared preferences
  • Server-side data is stored in a secure, access-controlled database
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only

6 Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. The App is used on behalf of children by their parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All account registration is completed by the parent or guardian.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and your child
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time (by deleting your account)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@insideout.app.

8 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Session reports are retained to allow ongoing specialist review. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. We will notify you of material changes through the App. Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10 Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at support@insideout.app.
